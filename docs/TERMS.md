## 1. What Hearth is

Hearth is an Alexa+ add-on (an MCP server) that remembers a household's appliances, shut-offs and repairs, walks you through the first 15 minutes of a home problem, finds parts that fit what you own, and prepares job briefs for professionals. This build is a hackathon submission. The Alexa+ device is a simulation, payments are test mode or simulated, and the recall and parts data are samples (see section 8). ## 3. DIY risk

Home repair can injure people and damage property. Hearth gives general guidance for common, low-risk jobs. It cannot see your home, and it can be wrong.

- You do the work at your own risk. You decide whether a step is safe for you, and you may stop at any time.

## 4. Hard stops

Some problems are never given DIY steps: gas, electrical work beyond resetting a breaker once (or a breaker that trips again), structural problems, active flooding beyond the reach of your shut-off, and sewage backing up in more than one fixture. Hearth also will not help with a child doing a repair, and will not store details about children. When a hard stop applies, Hearth reads a fixed message, gives no steps, and offers to prepare a brief for a professional. Asking again or insisting does not change this. The rules behind it are in `docs/SAFETY.md`. Hard stops are a safeguard, not a guarantee: they can miss, so you must still use your own judgement.

## 5. Orders and the commitment rule

- Hearth lists parts that appear to fit what you told it about your home. Fit is a best match from the data in your Home Graph and a demo catalogue; check the part against the one you remove. We cannot promise a part fits.
- Starting a checkout never charges you. Hearth first reads out exactly what you are committing to: the item, quantity, price, arrival date and payment method.
- An order is placed only after you clearly say yes (or confirm) after that is read to you. Silence, a question or an unclear answer is not a yes. Saying no cancels. A pending checkout expires after 30 minutes.
- Retrying a request does not create a second order or charge; each order has an order number and a receipt.
- Prices include tax at the rate shown when you confirm. In this build, shipping is free and tax is 8.25 percent.
- In this build no real payment is taken. Orders are paid in Stripe test mode with a test card, or fully simulated, and nothing is shipped. The confirmation says which. Should Hearth ever take real payments, these terms will be updated first and you will be told before your first real charge.
- Returns, refunds and disputes: write to support@hearth.example with your order number.

## 6. Pro briefs are informational

A pro brief is a summary of what you told Hearth and what it remembers: the item, its age, your description of the problem, a timeline, what you tried, access notes and photos. It is informational only.

- It is not a diagnosis, a quote, an estimate or a contract.
- Hearth does not select, vet, book, dispatch, guarantee or pay any professional, and is not a party to work you arrange. Any hand-off line to Alexa Home Services or another provider is an offer for you to accept or ignore, under that provider's terms.
- The brief only reflects what you said; a professional will confirm everything on site. Anyone with the link can read a brief, so share it deliberately.
